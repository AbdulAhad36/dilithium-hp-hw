   B. Overview of Dilithium
      Dilithium is a lattice-based digital signature scheme that
   achieves post-quantum security by relying on the hardness
   of the module learning with errors and module short integer
   solution problems [3]. All operations are defined over the
   polynomial ring Zq [x]/(xN + 1), where N = 256 and q is
   a carefully chosen prime to support efficient NTT implemen-
   tation.
      The key generation procedure begins by sampling a uni-
   formly random matrix A ∈ Zqk×l [x] from a public seed. Two
   secret vectors s1 ∈ Zl and s2 ∈ Zk are sampled from a
   centered binomial distribution χη . The public key is computed

   as t = A·s1 +s2 mod q, where all polynomial multiplications
   are performed efficiently using NTT.
