be chosen to be a prime such that q ≡ 1 mod N in order to

modulus must also satisfy q ≡ 1 mod 2N so that both the
N -th and 2N -th primitive roots of unity modulo q exist.

of ωN and ζN respectively to efficiently compute the transform
using a series of butterfly operations. There are two butterfly
